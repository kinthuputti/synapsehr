# Leave Policy (FICTIONAL demo document)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per year. Annual leave must be approved by the employee's manager. A single request cannot exceed 10 consecutive working days without HR approval.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Notify your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
Parental leave is handled by HR. Employees who expect to need parental leave should contact HR at least 8 weeks in advance. Requests are reviewed individually.

## Carry over
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried over days expire on 31 March.
