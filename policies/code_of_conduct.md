# Code of Conduct (FICTIONAL demo document)

## Respectful workplace
Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Employees who experience or witness such behaviour should report it to HR or through the confidential ethics hotline.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose any financial or personal interest that could conflict with company interests to their manager and HR.

## Gifts
Gifts from suppliers worth more than 50 USD must be declined or reported to HR.
