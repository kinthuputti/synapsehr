# Expense Policy (FICTIONAL demo document)

## Meals
Meal expenses during approved business travel are reimbursed up to 30 USD per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Hotel bookings must be made through the approved travel portal and are capped at 150 USD per night.

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with original receipts. Claims above 500 USD require approval from a department head.
