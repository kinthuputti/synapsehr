# Remote Work Policy (FICTIONAL demo document)

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period of 3 months may work remotely (work from home) up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop. Employees are responsible for a stable internet connection. Equipment must be returned when employment ends.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 local time on working days.

## Security
Company data must only be accessed on company devices over the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi must not be used without the VPN.
